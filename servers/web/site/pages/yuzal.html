Yuzal

An AI agent you can trust with your secrets

Yuzal lives in TizCat's side panel. She reads the page you are on and works with it for you, and she was designed so that your passwords never reach the AI, and so that, with the local engine, nothing leaves your laptop at all.

What she does

Your eyes and hands on the web, when you want them

Reads the page

Its text, its structure, and a screenshot when a picture says more than words.

Works with it

Clicks, types, scrolls, fills forms and attaches files, in a tab of her own so the one you read is never disturbed.

Runs task lists

A list of pages to read, a report to compile, a check to repeat every morning, with voice in and out if you like.

Your credentials stay on your laptop

Exactly what stays, and what leaves

  • Saved passwords never reach the AI. They stay in TizCat's password manager, encrypted for your Windows account. No extension can read it, and Yuzal is an extension. When a site asks you to sign in, she asks you to pick your saved account and the browser fills it.
  • Passwords never reach TizCat's servers. Even your TizCat account password never leaves the page you type it on: it is scrambled with 600,000 rounds first, and only the result is checked.
  • AI keys are never sent to TizCat. They stay in your settings and go only to the AI company you picked.
Two ways to think

Cloud AI for the hardest questions, the local engine for private documents

Yuzal works in two modes, and you switch between them in her panel at any time: Model, then "On this PC" for the local engine. Either way, passwords never reach the AI.

Cloud AI

  • A TizCat Cloud subscription: strong cloud models with no setup. Prices coming soon...
  • Your own key with Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude or another AI company. TizCat charges nothing for it, and the key goes only to that company.

For each turn, the page text and a screenshot go to that AI, under your agreement with it. Never your passwords.

The local engine, for private and highly secure documents

  • Free. An open model runs on your laptop through Ollama, with no bill and no account.
  • Nothing leaves. Bank statements, medical records, contracts and tax forms are read on your laptop and stay there.

About 16 GB of memory is recommended for the local engine.

Critical tasks

Banking, healthcare and other things that matter

What Yuzal is not

Yuzal is an assistant, not an adviser. AI can misread a page or be wrong, so check what she tells you before acting on it, especially with money or health. She is not a financial, medical or legal professional, and TizCat makes no claim of certification under health or banking regulations. The safeguards keep you in control; they do not replace your judgment.
