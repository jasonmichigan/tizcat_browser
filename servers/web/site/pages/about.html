About

Sharp, quick, and ready to move

The name TizCat comes from the Persian word tiz, meaning sharp and quick, and from the cheetah, the fastest animal on land. Like its namesake, TizCat is quick, alert, and ready to move. Whether you're researching, working, or just exploring, TizCat helps you move through the web with speed and focus.

Two cheetahs

The marks

The browser's mark is a gold cheetah head folded like paper, with navy tear lines: sharp edges, warm color, nothing soft about it. Yuzal's mark is a cheetah split down the middle, half spotted fur and half a teal network of connected points: the same animal, thinking.

Why a complete browser

Built properly, not disguised

TizCat began as Jeep, a browser built on an embedded browser framework. In October 2026 Google's sign-in refused Jeep, as it refuses every browser built that way, with the message that the browser "may not be secure". Complete, standalone browsers are not affected.

Rather than disguise Jeep to get past a security check, it was rebuilt properly as a complete browser. The work began on 29 October 2026, the browser was named TizCat on 16 November, and its accounts, Rewards and website went live on 18 November.
