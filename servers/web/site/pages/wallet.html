JibWallet

Your wallet, inside your browser, with your keys

JibWallet is TizCat's own wallet, designed as part of the browser: the place where JibCoin and other Solana assets are kept, received and spent.

Design principles

Built around self-custody

Your keys, your device

Keys are made and kept on your device, encrypted, and never sent to TizCat. TizCat cannot move, freeze or recover your funds.

A recovery phrase you control

Shown once, with clear guidance, and support for hardware wallets.

Readable confirmations

Every transaction is shown in plain words before you approve it: who receives what, and what it costs.

Phishing protection

Warnings for addresses and sites known to steal, and no silent approvals.

A separate module

JibWallet loads like TizCat's other modules, so it can be updated quickly and on its own.

Useful inside TizCat

Spend JibCoin on AI credits or a premium plan without ever converting it to cash.
