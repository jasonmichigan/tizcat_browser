Yuzal

An AI agent you can trust with your bank and your health

An agent that can click and type is only useful for important tasks if it cannot do the wrong thing on its own. Yuzal is built around three rules.

  • Passwords never reach the AI. You pick your saved account; the browser fills it in.
  • The local engine keeps everything home. The page, the screenshot and your words never leave your laptop.
  • You press the button. Read-only and never-submit keep payments, transfers and messages in your hands.
