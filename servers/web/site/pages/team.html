The people behind TizCat

An academic team building a fast browser with an AI agent people can trust, and a business that never needs to sell anyone's data.

Ramona

Business Manager ramona@tizcat.com

Ramona leads business development at TizCat. She builds and manages the company's commercial partnerships, from shopping and travel partners to the advertisers who reach TizCat users through sponsored shortcuts and suggestions, and through sponsored New Tab pictures and notifications in TizCat Rewards. Companies interested in advertising with TizCat work directly with Ramona. She also oversees TizCat's operations, finance, and legal and regulatory affairs, and represents TizCat to its partners and the press.

Hamid

Developer Manager hamid@tizcat.com

Hamid leads how TizCat is built. He sets the technical direction for the browser, from its speed-optimized build to Yuzal, TizCat's AI agent, and the servers behind TizCat accounts and Rewards. His rule for the engineering is that privacy is designed in, not added later. He oversees releases, security reviews and the technology roadmap for JibWallet.
