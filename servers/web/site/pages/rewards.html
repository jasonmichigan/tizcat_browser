TizCat Rewards

Earn from your attention, on your terms

Advertisers pay to reach people. With TizCat Rewards, the people who choose to see ads keep most of what advertisers pay, and their browsing never leaves their browser. Rewards is off until you turn it on.

How it works

Four steps, all in your control

Turn it on

Open TizCat Rewards from the toolbar and switch it on. No Rewards ad is shown or counted before you do.

See ads your way

A sponsored picture on some New Tab pages (one in four by default) and a few notifications an hour, from none to ten, only while you are at the computer.

Matched on your device

TizCat downloads the list of running ads and picks one inside your browser. Your history is never sent anywhere for ads.

Points, counted once

Each view has its own random id, so it counts once. Points are kept in your TizCat account.

Never inside other sites

TizCat shows sponsored content only in its own places: the New Tab page, the address bar and its own notifications. It never inserts or replaces ads on the web pages you visit.

Sponsored shortcuts, suggestions and partner bookmarks

Besides Rewards, TizCat earns from sponsored shortcuts and address-bar suggestions, each labeled Sponsored, and from partner bookmarks for shopping and travel sites. These are separate from Rewards points. How they appear and how to switch them off: Details coming soon...

What you can expect

Honest numbers

Your share is 70 percent of what advertisers actually paid for your views. At a realistic five ads a day and five dollars per thousand views, that is about 53 cents a month, in line with what Brave users report. The only way to reach a few dollars a month is twenty ads a day, which is too many for anyone.

Rewards is a thank-you for attention, not an income to live on. Its real value is that TizCat never needs to sell your data to pay its way.

Fair and protected

Built so it lasts

Paid only from real money

Payouts are designed so that everyone's balances plus everything paid out can never exceed 70 percent of what advertisers actually paid. If a bug ever broke that rule, payouts would stop.

Limits

A daily cap on counted events per device and a minimum payout, so mistakes and abuse cannot drain the pool.

Fraud controls before any payout

Identity checks only when someone withdraws, never when they earn; a payout delay with a window to reverse fraud; detection of data-center addresses and mass-produced profiles.

Delete everything, anytime

Deleting your TizCat account deletes its linked browsers, its sessions and its points.

From points to payouts

Points, payouts and JibCoin

Rewards counts points in your TizCat account, on a ledger built and tested to balance. Payouts are designed in two forms: gift cards or a dollar stablecoin, with identity checks at withdrawal, and points that convert to JibCoin, held in JibWallet.
