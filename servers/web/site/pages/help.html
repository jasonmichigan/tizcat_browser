Common questions about TizCat, your account, Yuzal and Rewards.

How do I create a TizCat account?

Go to account.tizcat.com and sign up with your email and a password of at least 10 characters. In TizCat, open the same page to link your browser to the account, so your Rewards points are kept.

How do I import from another browser?

In TizCat, open Settings, then Profile, then "Import bookmarks and settings", and pick the browser and profile. Passwords that Chrome or Edge lock to themselves cannot be read by another program; export them from that browser's password manager as a file and import the file instead.

Does Yuzal see my passwords?

No. Saved passwords stay in TizCat's password manager, which no extension can read. When a site asks you to sign in, Yuzal asks you to pick your saved account and the browser fills it in.

How do I keep everything on my computer?

Choose the local engine in Yuzal's settings ("On this PC"). It runs an open model through Ollama on your own computer, so the page and your words never leave it. Turn on read-only when you only want Yuzal to look.

Is the AI really free?

Yuzal works two ways. The local engine on your computer is free, and it is the one to use for private or highly secure documents, because nothing leaves your laptop. For cloud AI, you can connect your own Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude or other key, which TizCat charges nothing for, or choose a TizCat Cloud subscription for strong cloud models with no setup. How does TizCat make money?

From five sources, and never from selling your data: TizCat Rewards ads (viewers keep 70 percent), sponsored shortcuts and address-bar suggestions, partner bookmarks for shopping and travel sites, the TizCat Cloud subscription, and a small fee on token swaps in JibWallet. How much can I earn with Rewards?

About 53 cents a month at five ads a day, which is 70 percent of what advertisers pay for those views. Rewards is a thank-you for attention, not an income.

What are JibCoin and JibWallet?

JibWallet is TizCat's self-custody wallet: your keys stay on your device. JibCoin is TizCat's reward token, designed to be earned by the attention you choose to give to ads and spent inside TizCat on AI. The whitepaper sets out its design.

How do I delete my account?

Log in at account.tizcat.com and choose "Delete my account". Its linked browsers, sessions and points are deleted with it.
