Principles

Utility first, a closed loop, honest words

Utility first

JibCoin is built to do something a dollar cannot do as well inside TizCat.

A closed loop

Advertisers pay for attention, attention earns JibCoin, JibCoin pays for AI. Nothing has to leave the loop.

Fixed supply

No token minted for every view; a large share reserved for the people who earn it.

Honest words

Never described as an investment, never given a price prediction, never promised a listing.

Token design

Simple, standard, auditable

NetworkSolana, a standard SPL token; transfers cost a fraction of a cent
ContractThe standard, unmodified token program, no custom code
SupplyFixed: no tokens are added after the supply is minted
Freeze authorityDisabled: no one, including TizCat, can freeze holders' tokens
AuditIndependent, before deployment
